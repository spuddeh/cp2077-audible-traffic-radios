# Changelog

### [Unreleased - v0.1.0]
- Radios in traffic cars play, and you can hear them, through the game's own car radio sound.
- Other radios keep playing while your own car radio is on.
- Each traffic car plays at its own level, most near the middle and a few quiet or loud, and keeps it.
- From inside a car in first person, traffic radios are muffled the way the game muffles traffic noise.
- A traffic car with a door open or torn off, a window down or broken, or a convertible sounds more open: the
  more of it is open, the less muffled and the louder it is, keeping its own car-class sound. It goes back to the
  muffled sound when it closes up.
- Walls muffle traffic radios the way they muffle the game's other radios.
